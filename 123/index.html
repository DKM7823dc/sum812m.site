Emotional Manipulation

Romance Scam

Fake personas on dating apps build trust, then request "emergency" funds.

The scammer spent 6 months building a relationship, then asked for $50,000 in Bitcoin for a "medical emergency." We traced the wallet and alerted exchanges to freeze the funds.

Investment Scam

Fake Wealth Management Platform

Fraudsters use fake investment apps and social media ads to promise unrealistically high returns, then freeze accounts and block withdrawals.

A retiree transferred $50,000 to a cloned trading platform after seeing a celebrity-endorsed ad. Our system flagged the domain as suspicious within 5 minutes and stopped the second transfer of $30,000.

On‑Chain Fraud

Fake DeFi projects execute rug pulls, draining millions from investors.

A "revolutionary" token project rugged $15M in ETH within 48 hours. Our on-chain analysis identified the deployer wallet and helped freeze remaining assets.

Bitcoin Scam

Fake mining platforms promise huge returns — then vanish with deposits.

Altcoins

Crypto Fraud

Pump-and-dump schemes crash tokens after insiders cash out.

A Telegram group with 50K members promoted a "guaranteed" token that crashed 99% in 24 hours. Our team identified the insider wallets and issued a public alert.

Task Scam

Click‑Farming Rebate Fraud

Scammers promise high commissions for simple online tasks, then demand prepayments and disappear with the funds.

A victim earned small rebates on the first few orders, then was asked to pay $8,000 for a "premium task package". The platform froze withdrawals immediately after the transfer. Our team helped freeze the receiving account and recovered 70% of the loss.
