FAQ

What is TRX energy?
Energy is the TRON resource for smart contracts. TRC20 transfers consume it; if you have none, TRX is burned as fees.
Is renting safe?
Rental is on-chain stake delegation. We only grant energy usage to your address and cannot move your assets. Verify on a block explorer.
How fast does it land?
Usually 1–3 seconds after payment. The desk runs 24/7 with no manual review.
How long does it last?
About 1 hour per rental. Complete your transfer as soon as energy arrives.
Which wallets work?
Any wallet that can send TRX: TronLink, TokenPocket, imToken, Trust Wallet, BitKeep and others.
